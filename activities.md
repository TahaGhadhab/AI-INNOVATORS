1. 🌿 AI Nature Detective — EASIEST

This is probably the best starting project.

Build a website:

📸 Upload a photo of something in nature

AI returns:

Species: Olive tree
Category: Plant
Confidence: 94%
Interesting fact: ...
Environmental importance: ...

Resources

You essentially need:

laptops
internet
an AI vision API/model
simple web application

No hardware. No sensors. No dataset creation required.

You could even make it a club competition:

“Can AI identify Tunisia's biodiversity?”

🥈 Plant Doctor 🌱

This is another extremely accessible one.

User uploads:

📷 leaf.jpg

AI returns:

🟢 Plant appears healthy

or

🟠 Possible nutrient deficiency detected

or

🔴 Possible fungal disease

Then show educational information.

Why I like this for a club

Different members can work on:

frontend
AI integration
UX/UI
database
research
testing

So you don't need everyone to be an AI specialist.

🥉 AI Beach Pollution Detector 🏖️

This one has much stronger presentation potential.

Take a beach photograph:

             📷
              ↓
       AI analyzes image
              ↓
 ┌─────────────────────────┐
 │ Plastic bottles:    12  │
 │ Plastic bags:        7  │
 │ Cans:                4  │
 │ Other waste:         3  │
 └─────────────────────────┘
              ↓
     Pollution Score: 68/100

You could create a simple dashboard showing:

number of objects
pollution score
waste categories
before/after cleanup

And the club could actually collect photos from local beaches.

Very little physical equipment is needed.

4. ♻️ AI Waste Classifier

Very similar technically, but even easier.

Upload an image:

🥤 Plastic bottle

AI says:

Category: Plastic
Recyclable: Yes
Bin: Plastic
Environmental tip: ...

You can make the interface extremely simple.

Optional physical version

If you later want to make it more impressive, add:

webcam
Arduino/ESP32
servo

But don't start with hardware.

The first version can be entirely software.

5. 🏙️ AI Green City

This is probably the least technically demanding.

Give AI a city scenario:

Population: 500,000
Traffic: High
Green spaces: Low
Pollution: High

AI proposes:

🌳 More green areas
🚲 Bike lanes
🚌 Public transport
☀️ Solar installations
♻️ Recycling infrastructure

Then use generative AI to visualize the proposed city.

This is particularly good for a workshop rather than a serious software project.
